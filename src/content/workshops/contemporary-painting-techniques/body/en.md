Contemporary painting has evolved dramatically in recent decades, with artists exploring new materials, techniques and conceptual approaches. This masterclass delves into the cutting-edge methods that define modern artistic practice.

From mixed-media applications to experimental brushwork, participants will learn how today's artists push the boundaries of traditional painting. We'll explore techniques such as impasto, glazing and the use of unconventional materials that create unique textures and visual effects.

The workshop includes hands-on practice sessions where you'll experiment with various techniques under expert guidance. Whether you're an experienced painter or looking to expand your skills, this masterclass offers valuable insights into contemporary artistic practice.
