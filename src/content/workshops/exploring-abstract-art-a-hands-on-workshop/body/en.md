Abstract art has long been a profound expression of creativity, where artists break away from traditional representation to explore the boundaries of colour, form and emotion. The focus is not on depicting reality, but on conveying ideas and emotions through non-representational means.

From the bold strokes of Wassily Kandinsky to the geometric precision of Piet Mondrian, abstract art has evolved over the decades, influencing countless artists and movements. It invites viewers to interpret the work through their own perceptions and feelings, making it a deeply personal experience.

In this workshop we delve into the history and evolution of abstract art, highlighting key artists and their contributions to this revolutionary genre. We also explore how contemporary artists continue to push the boundaries of abstraction, using innovative techniques and materials to create works that challenge our understanding of art itself.
